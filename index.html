FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

How long does it take to build a website? +

Typically, a standard website takes 2–4 weeks. More complex projects like e-commerce or custom web apps may take 6–12 weeks depending on requirements.

Do you offer post-launch support? +

Yes! We offer 3 months of free support after launch. After that, you can choose one of our maintenance plans to keep your site updated and secure.

Will my website be mobile friendly? +

Absolutely! All websites we build are fully responsive and optimized for mobile, tablet and desktop devices.

Can I update the website content myself? +

Yes! We build websites with easy-to-use content management systems (CMS) so you can update text, images and other content without any coding knowledge.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept bKash, Nagad, bank transfer and all major credit/debit cards. We also offer flexible payment plans for larger projects.

Do you offer SEO services with web design? +

Yes! Every website we build is SEO-ready from day one. We also offer dedicated SEO packages to help you rank higher on Google and drive more organic traffic.
